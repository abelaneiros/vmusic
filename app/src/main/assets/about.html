A fork of Vanilla Music

vMusic is a modified version of Vanilla Music by Adrian Ulrich and contributors, based on upstream commit 38e17613 (version 1.3.2, released 10. Jan. 2024).

Modified by Abel ANEIROS in 2026. Changes include the History tab, Maven build, minSdk 26, the reworked permission flow, English and Spanish only, and a new icon.

Please report vMusic issues to the vMusic issue tracker, not to the Vanilla Music project.

Original project: https://vanilla-music.github.io/

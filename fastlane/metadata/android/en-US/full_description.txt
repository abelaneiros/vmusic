vMusic is a simple and lightweight MP3/OGG/FLAC/PCM player for the music stored on your device.

Features:
* History tab: the last 10 queues you played, with their track and position, so you can resume any of them. Pin the ones you want to keep.
* multiple playlist support
* grouping by artist, album or genre
* plain filesystem browsing
* ReplayGain support
* headset/Bluetooth controls
* accelerometer/shake control
* cover art support
* Simple Last.fm Scrobbler support

vMusic is a fork of Vanilla Music by Adrian Ulrich and contributors. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Vanilla Music project.
